Elasticity 2.0 metamodel

Successor design to SPD 1.0: a scalable architecture (plant) plus policies with pluggable decision procedures (reactive, learning, scheduled). Source: model/Elasticity.ecore.

Viewpoint structure

Root splits plant (ScalableArchitecture) from controllers (ElasticityPolicySet). Policies reference groups; they do not own PCM topology.

Scalable groups replace SPD TargetGroup subclasses

Instead of ElasticInfrastructure / ServiceGroup / CompetingConsumersGroup, a group composes cardinality + architectural binding + optional enactment hints.
