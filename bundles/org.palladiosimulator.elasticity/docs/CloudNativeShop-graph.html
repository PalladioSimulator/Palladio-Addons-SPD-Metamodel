Do targets share PCM elements?

No shared membership. Each ScalableGroup owns one binding to a unit (container template or assembly). Containers and assemblies are not “in” multiple targets.

Infra targets scale hosts; service / consumer targets scale assemblies. Placement of service replicas onto app VMs is allocation (dotted), not a shared target.
